Official Contest Rules

Last updated: November 2025

1. Nature of Contests

Player Pops hosts skill-based contests in which participants make predictions about the performance of NFL and NBA players using virtual $PP. Outcomes depend on the actual on-field statistics of the players involved, not on chance alone.

2. Prop Markets (Above/Below)

Each prop is a binary Above/Below market priced by an Automated Market Maker (AMM). The displayed Above and Below percentages always sum to 100%. Buying a side shifts the percentages in proportion to the size of the trade. Early cash-out is available at the live market percentage until the contest locks.

3. Tournaments

Daily NBA and NFL tournaments open on days with more than one scheduled game. Entries lock at the start time of the first game of that sport. The first-place prize pool starts at 2,500 $PP and grows by 750 $PP for each entry. In the event of a cancellation or postponement that voids all games in a tournament, affected entries are refunded in full.

4. Phase Challenges

The 2-Step ($25) and 3-Step ($20) challenges require participants to meet per-phase profit targets, minimum wins, maximum losses, and drawdown limits using the same Above/Below prop markets. Breaching any loss cap or drawdown limit auto-fails the challenge. Completing all phases credits the completion reward specified in the app.

5. Referral Program

Referrers receive 1,000 $PP after a referred user makes a qualifying purchase of $5 or more. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts, and fraudulent referrals will be reversed, and the referring account may be suspended.

6. Settlement and Corrections

Contests settle based on official statistics from the respective league or a licensed data provider. In the event of an obvious statistical error, Player Pops reserves the right to re-settle the affected contests using the corrected data. Prop markets may be voided (with full refund of stakes) if a game is cancelled or a player does not participate.

7. Eligibility and Restricted States

The following states are restricted and may not participate: WA, MT, ID, NV, LA, HI. State eligibility is verified at tournament entry, pack purchase, and challenge start. Attempting to bypass these restrictions is grounds for immediate termination.

8. No Gambling

Player Pops is a skill-based contest platform. $PP are virtual entertainment credits with no cash value. Nothing in the Service constitutes gambling, betting, or wagering for real money.

9. Sponsorship Disclaimer

Apple Inc. and Google LLC are not sponsors, endorsers, or administrators of any Player Pops contest. Any questions, complaints, or disputes must be directed to Player Pops at support@playerpopsapp.com, not to Apple or Google.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Player Pops ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing.

State location for eligibility verification

Because Player Pops contests are not available in certain states (WA, MT, ID, NV, LA, HI), we verify your state of residence when you enter a tournament, purchase a pack, or start a challenge. This verification uses:

  • Device geolocation — with your permission, we reverse-geocode your coarse location to a US state. We do not store precise coordinates.
  • IP-based lookup — if device geolocation is unavailable or denied, we query a third-party IP geolocation service to determine your approximate state.

Only the resulting two-letter state code is retained for eligibility audit purposes. Precise GPS coordinates are discarded immediately after lookup.

Activity and transaction data

We maintain a ledger of your activity to operate the Service, including Pop Points balance, wagers placed, tournament entries, challenge progress, store purchases, and referral events. This data is retained as part of your account history and is used to settle contests, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable law.

When you purchase Pop Points through the mobile app (Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing) or this webstore (Stripe), we receive a transaction record from the payment processor containing the product purchased, the price, and a unique transaction ID. We do not receive or store your full payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple, Google, or Stripe.
